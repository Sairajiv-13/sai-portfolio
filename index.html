03 / ENGINEERING PHILOSOPHY

Proof over buzzwords.

The standards I try to bring to AI engineering projects, made checkable in public code.

01 — EVALUATE

Measure failure, not just success.

Baselines, representative test sets, error categories and regression checks make claims auditable. A passing sandbox evaluation is not a production-quality guarantee.

02 — CONSTRAIN

Put safety in the system.

Tenant isolation, typed tools, idempotency, budget controls and human escalation belong in architecture, not only prompts.

03 — DISCLOSE

Say what isn't proven.

Distinguish working local demos from production systems. Explain tradeoffs, measurement gaps and rejected design alternatives.
